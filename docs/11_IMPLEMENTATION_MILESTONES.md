# Implementation Milestones

## Milestone 1 — Foundation

Packs 01–02: repo, database bootstrap, master data, IAM, workflow, case/decision/licensing. Output: secure officer login, master registries, workflow/task queue, basic regulatory case lifecycle.

## Milestone 2 — Regulatory products/facilities

Pack 03: facility establishment, factory/GMP, veterinary products and feed. Output: facility 360, product 360, registration/licence foundations.

## Milestone 3 — Inspection & laboratory

Pack 04. Output: mobile-ready inspections, findings/CAPA/enforcement, samples, chain of custody, validated laboratory results.

## Milestone 4 — Traceability & animal health

Packs 05–06. Output: farm/animal identity, movement, disease surveillance, outbreak, vaccination, restrictions.

## Milestone 5 — Trade & fisheries

Pack 07. Output: operator/permit/shipment/border/quarantine/clearance/e-certificate and aquaculture records.

## Milestone 6 — National platform hardening

Pack 08. Output: GIS layers, dashboards/KPIs, notification, integration registry, scheduler, data quality, observability and production readiness.

## Release path

Development → Staging → Pilot/UAT → Production. Do not deploy all domains nationally in one untested cutover; enable capability groups progressively with feature flags/configuration where appropriate.
